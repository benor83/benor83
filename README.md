## ⚔️ SPACE MARINE VR — *Anniversary Edition*

Fifteen years ago Relic sealed us in the armour of **Captain Titus of the Ultramarines** and sent us
down onto the forge world **Graia**, into a greenskin tide, with a chainsword and no cover to hide
behind. It remains the only game that understood what an Astartes is meant to *feel* like.

It was played, however, from over his shoulder. A painting of a battle rather than the battle.

I have spent these months ending that.

### Proven in the headset

| | |
|---|---|
| ⚙️ **True stereo** | Both eyes rendered by the engine itself. No depth-map trickery. |
| ⚙️ **The body is yours** | Look down and the plate is on *your* chest. Titus's skeleton is driven outright, bone by bone — not patched over the game's animation. |
| ⚙️ **Your hands are your hands** | Chainsword and bolter welded to them. You aim down the barrel with your arm. |
| ⚙️ **The blade rides your hip** | And comes free when you reach for it. |
| ⚙️ **The Rite of Arming** | You kneel. A Techmarine speaks the litany. You rise and answer aloud — *"For the Emperor"* — and stand in the cruciform whilst the armour is measured to your body. |
| ⚙️ **And the greenskins** | Close your fist upon one and he is yours. Lift him squirming on your palm. Then throw him — **fourteen metres**, measured. His landing was not gentle. |

**Status:** in progress. **No date** — it ships when it is worthy.
